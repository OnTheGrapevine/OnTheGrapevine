OVERVIEW

since i fixed an old sewing machine for me to use and started attending The Preloved Studio by The Wheel Merton, i needed a beginner project to start on and this seemed like an easy and safe option whilst still being functional and a cool addition to my daily outfits. hoping that this utility belt will help me hold tools whilst working on other projects and repairs in the future.

notable parts:

  • second hand black fabric

LOGBOOK

  • sketched a design for the flashlight pocket. Eugene advised on better dimensions such as including more leeway for the belt loop and pocket and additional length to overlap folds and clean edges. i then redrew the design. Fiona advised me on how to choose fabric. Eugene taught me how to measure and cut the fabric. Fiona taught me how to clean the edges via using an overlocking machine and then sewing lines to fold over the edges. I was able to complete overlocking the edges - it wasn't perfect, but I'm more than happy for my first try. Attempted to sew my first line but struggled getting a neat fold. I learnt how to use an unpicker. -13/10/2026 @ The Preloved Studio (by The Wheel Merton)
  • i finished doing lines to fold over the edges. i did another line to fold over the belt loop - it fits but is very tight so i might redo it, i'm pretty sure there is enough length to make the belt loop bigger. -17/10/2026 @ The Wheel Merton
